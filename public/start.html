Get started with Precedents

Greenshoot's shared library of built-project precedents. Anyone on the team can send precedents in from the web, check what Groundwork drafts, and search the whole collection. It takes about two minutes to set up.

  1. Open the library

    Go to and sign in with the team password (ask Ed) and your name. Your name goes on everything you add or approve. Bookmark the page.

  2. Add the Send button to your browser

    Browser extension (Chrome or Edge, on Mac or Windows): coming soon. Until then, use the bookmark button:

    Bookmark button (any browser): show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows, Cmd+Shift+B on Mac), then drag this button onto it.

    Send to Precedents

    On a project page (ArchDaily, a practice website, a news story), highlight any useful text, then click the button. Tick the images worth keeping, add a note, and send.

  3. Optional: the Mac desktop widget

    For screenshots and text from PDFs and other apps on a Mac. Ask Ed for the Groundwork Capture app.

How it works

1. Send

From a web page, or upload your own site photos on the Add page.

2. Groundwork drafts

It reads the page and images, fills in the details, chooses the categories and checks the library for duplicates.

3. Anyone approves

Open the Inbox, check the draft, fix anything, and approve. It's then in the library, credited to whoever sent and approved it.

4. Search

Filter by category, search by keyword, or Ask Groundwork in plain words. Open any precedent and use Find similar.
